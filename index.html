I’m Jonathan, an AI engineer focused on developing intelligent systems that perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world.

My background spans computer vision, 3D perception, reinforcement learning, simulation, and high-performance edge AI. I’ve led the development of end-to-end ML systems—from synthetic data generation and distributed training to inference optimization and deployment on real-world platforms.

I’m particularly interested in world models, embodied intelligence, and learning-based autonomy, with a long-term focus on advancing general-purpose AI and its applications in robotics, scientific discovery, and human well-being.
